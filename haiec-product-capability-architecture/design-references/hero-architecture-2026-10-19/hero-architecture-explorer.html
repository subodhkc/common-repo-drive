Why the architecture is different

Capability is not authority

What code may be able to do is distinct from what policy permits and connected identities actually grant.

Observation is not assumption

Source reachability, declared intent and runtime evidence remain separate. Unknown or not assessed is not a pass.

Decision is not enforcement

Human approvals and assurance dispositions help govern change. A native guard must be connected and independently proven.
