Questions enterprise buyers ask

What the solution can establish.

How is HAIEC different from ordinary model or code testing?

Individual tests can provide valuable component findings. HAIEC focuses on supported consequential action paths and examines policy, effective access, decision authority and operating evidence within the evaluated scope. It complements rather than replaces existing observability, testing and governance systems.

Can HAIEC identify risk before all identity and telemetry integrations are connected?

Yes. Eligible source analysis can reveal action-capable routes and relevant technical findings. Additional IAM, policy and runtime facts answer specific stronger claims rather than serving as a blanket requirement for the first finding.

Do the five evidence views represent a strict sequence?

No. Requested, Policy Authorized, Effectively Granted, Code Capable and Observed are independent views of an action. HAIEC only uses the views relevant and available for the selected question and represents their exact scope.

What does Delegated Action Integrity add?

It helps distinguish technical permission from the business authority to make a consequential choice. The decision may be determined by approved policy or depend on bounded authority delegated to the agent.

Is Enterprise Assurance POC the same as the ASSURE subscription?

No. The Enterprise POC is a qualified, separately scoped technical engagement with its own readiness, access, timeline and contracted outputs. Platform subscriptions are a different commercial path.

Does HAIEC certify clients on behalf of assurance firms?

No. HAIEC supplies supported technical evidence, analysis and reviewer workflows. A qualified audit, assurance or certification organization retains its applicable independent professional judgment and final issuance.

Can HAIEC automatically block every consequential AI action?

Only an installed, authorized and correctly operating enforcement integration can stop a real target-side action. Evaluated policy, control configuration, report dispositions and actual blocked effects are separate facts.

How are Telecom AL0, AL1 and AL2 states interpreted?

They are declared baseline, degraded and compromised scenario states used to organize evidence comparison. The state label alone is not proof of attacker intent, verified compromise or causal effect. Qualified source and event records support those claims.

Can Advisory Partners deliver HAIEC assurance independently?

The Advisory Partner program is a separate qualified-introduction relationship. HAIEC retains responsibility for enterprise qualification and delivery under that program. Professional Assurance Firm engagements have their own scope and responsibilities.

What should my team prepare for a first enterprise conversation?

A short description of the AI system, consequential business action, decision owner, approved source/access boundaries and the decision you need to defend. HAIEC can help scope the minimum useful evidence and identify a first technical finding.
