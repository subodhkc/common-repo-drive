Frequently asked questions

From source findings to decisions with evidence.

What does HAIEC analyze first?

Begin with an authorized AI system, eligible source and consequential question. Supported source analysis can find potential action paths before every provider is connected.

Can HAIEC find issues using repository evidence alone?

It can identify source-supported possible capabilities and derived paths in analyzed code. Confirming actual runtime execution is a separate evidence question.

Do I have to connect all five evidence views?

No. They answer independent questions. The selected claim determines which facts are necessary for a stronger conclusion.

How does HAIEC differ from an AI security scanner?

It connects relevant component findings to consequential action paths, policy, effective access and delegated choice, then supports deterministic evidence-backed assurance.

Does tool permission authorize a business decision?

Not always. Business discretion can be different from technical access. A rule-grounded choice or authorized bounded delegation can establish specific decision rights.

Does another AI model issue the verdict?

No. A configured AI assistant can guide the workflow, while HAIEC's deterministic evaluator owns the canonical bounded conclusion.

What do ALLOW, REVIEW and BLOCK mean?

These dispositions apply within their actual evaluated scope and contract. They do not by themselves assert universal safety, certification or target-side enforcement.

Does HAIEC continuously monitor or stop all agents?

Monitoring depends on supported connected sources, and prevention needs an installed, separately verified customer-side intervention path.

How can I verify a report?

Review the persisted source identity, evaluated policy, evidence and any available integrity receipt. Verification is bounded by that specific evaluated basis.

How can I begin an enterprise evaluation?

Scope an Enterprise POC, or connect through MCP, authorized GitHub, APIs or the workspace. Begin with one consequential system and a question your sources can answer.
