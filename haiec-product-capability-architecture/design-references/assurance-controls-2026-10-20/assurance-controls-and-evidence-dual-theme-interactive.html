The control question enterprises actually need answered

Having a control isn't the same as knowing it worked.

Compliance mappings describe expectations. A visibility finding concerns a specific source and control objective. A recorded test describes an actual evaluated scope. A protection claim requires evidence that the control really operated. These are different facts: and HAIEC helps teams keep them straight.

01 / Defined

Cataloged

What control objective exists? This does not mean the customer's system implements it.

02 / Source

Visible

What does an eligible source or bounded scan actually establish, and for which control?

03 / Test

Evaluated

Was an actual control test performed with applicable scope, approvals and recorded result?

04 / Effect

Operating

Did a connected target-side control trigger or mediate an action, supported by effect evidence?

Cataloged ≠ Implemented ≠ Evaluated ≠ Effective. This isn't a mandatory four-step gate for displaying an earlier meaningful source finding.

Evidence quality

Versioned facts, not impressions

Source, identity, time, coverage, policy basis and exact test/record version matter. A hash binds an artifact; it cannot prove the business effect.

Approval and replay

A recorded decision isn't enforcement

An approval or replay can establish a bounded governance or consistency fact. It doesn't automatically establish that a customer-side execution point honored it.

Real operating proof

Don't infer the block

To claim prevention, verify an installed mediation path, trigger, action and relevant target/effect witness. “Control available” isn't “control worked.”

Frequently asked questions

Know the difference between a control and its proof.

What are HAIEC Assurance Controls?

A system-oriented catalog of bounded control questions around agent authority, application/tool security, runtime consequences, governance, evidence, LLM reliability and responsible AI. The catalog itself doesn't mean every control is active, tested or effective.

How is this different from the existing Controls and Framework Registry?

The existing /controls page browses mapped regulatory and framework requirements. HAIEC's 45-entry assurance catalog is a separate system-focused inventory for assessing what supported evidence and tests establish about consequential AI actions.

Does listing a control mean it was deployed or worked?

No. Catalog definition, visibility implementation, recorded test result and proven target-side operating effect each answer different questions.

Can HAIEC find meaningful risks before all evidence sources are connected?

Yes for eligible source-based technical findings. A reachable consequential handler or candidate exposure can be worth investigation while IAM, telemetry or control test evidence is incomplete. Specific stronger verdicts require their own applicable basis.

What does “No finding observed” prove?

Only that no matching finding was observed within the specific completed and qualified scan scope. It doesn't prove that no risk exists or that any runtime protection operated.

Is a Control Visibility state the same as a Control Test verdict?

No. Visibility is a bounded read projection. A Control Test needs an actual eligible execution and persisted result. HAIEC's canonical Assurance evaluation is another distinct claim type.

What does a recorded Control Test prove?

It establishes the recorded test's bounded conclusion under its frozen policy, test contract, evidence and scope. It doesn't automatically establish continuous production enforcement.

How do you demonstrate an AI control really operated?

Verify an integrated target-side decision/mediation path and relevant trigger and effect records. A declaration, approval or integrity hash is not proof that an external target was blocked.

How many controls are available?

The reviewed source has 45 total catalog entries: 42 profiles and three specialized tests. Fifteen are tagged launch priority and 11 IDs have Control Visibility projection support. These numbers do not mean 45 runnable production protections.

How does DAI relate to assurance controls?

Delegated Action Integrity addresses whether a consequential choice falls within supported delegated authority. It can inform applicable control questions, alongside the independent policy, granted, code-capable and observed evidence views.

Do I have to upload policies or runtime logs before HAIEC can discover an issue?

No blanket upload requirement should suppress supported source-based findings. Evidence is connected as needed for the exact question, test or operational effect being claimed.

Can my AI agent use HAIEC controls through MCP?

A compatible configured MCP client can guide authorized HAIEC workflows. Actual control-specific MCP tool availability, entitlements, system/source eligibility and permissions must be verified; a public demo can't authorize execution.
