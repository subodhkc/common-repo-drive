Does the POC require production access?

No blanket production access is required. The SOW determines the minimum evidence needed, and active tests require written authorization.

Does HAIEC guarantee a local/offline deployment?

Do not assume it. Data residency and deployment modes must be technically qualified and contractually scoped before they are promised for an engagement.

Is this compliance certification?

No. It is a bounded technical assessment and executive decision-support engagement, not independent legal certification.
