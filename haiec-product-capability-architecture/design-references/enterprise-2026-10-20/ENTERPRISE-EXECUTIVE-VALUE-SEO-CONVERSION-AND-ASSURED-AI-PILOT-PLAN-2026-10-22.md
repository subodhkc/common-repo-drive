### Commercial architecture: three entry options, two paid POC routes
1. **Executive Evidence Demo** — show a source-qualified Kestrel sample and a guided decision dossier. Low-friction request; no promise of a free customer-specific assessment.
2. **Enterprise Assurance POC** — evaluate ONE existing customer AI application/agent or selected backlog POC already available for assessment, scoped evidence and authority, decision output; keep current $25K–$75K typical range and 4–8 weeks after readiness, governed by SOW.
3. **Assured AI Pilot Lab** (recommended LIMITED OFFER) — for buyers with a meaningful use case but no deployable AI system, or who do not want initial work in their environment. HAIEC/approved delivery partner builds one bounded use-case prototype in a dedicated sandbox using synthetic, public, or explicitly approved deidentified inputs, then HAIEC applies its deterministic evidence-bound assessment. Deliver an executable demonstrator, acceptance/test inventory, control/authority boundary, evidenced report, unresolved gaps and a transition recommendation. Not a production build contract, managed services promise, or substitute for real-environment assurance.

Do **not** call it an "independent audit" when HAIEC also built the sandbox POC. Separate prototype-builder artifacts, frozen evidence, deterministic evaluator inputs/rule versions and named human reviewer; an external reviewer can be contracted if true independent assurance is desired. Do not use "certified" or "production ready" based on sandbox evaluation. The final customer production decision still needs environment-specific identity, integrations, data/privacy approval, performance/reliability and possibly live observations.

Avoid pricing the build route by analogy to the existing Assurance POC: price and schedule only after a delivery feasibility review. Internal early hypothesis: small fixed-scope, time-boxed paid discovery/build engagement with optional assurance stage and separately agreed production transition; this is NOT a published price/timeline promise.

- No unrestricted customer credential, operational system access or uncontrolled personal data;

## Executive value model

The executive buys measurable **decision readiness**, not assurances of zero risk. Group metrics as follows:

| Value category | Customer-facing metric or artifact | Measurement / rigor |
|---|---|---|
| Consequential exposure | Number of code-capable action paths by consequence and resource, and which are qualified reachable | Counts only from defined system/source/commit; do not describe code-capable as executed or inherently vulnerable |
| Authority divergence | Number of supported policy-vs-grant-vs-delegation contradictions and affected workflows | Distinguish established contrary evidence from missing evidence |
| Evidence coverage | Scoped claims that have sufficient qualified proof / total applicable scoped claims; independently show partial, unknown, not assessed | Publish numerator, denominator, scope, source, freshness; no control-catalog count disguised as coverage |
| Decision backlog | Material unresolved questions by owner, consequence, due action and verification requirement | Unknowns are not silent passes; show before/after in repeat review |
| Determinism / replay | Result and evidence digest comparison when the **same frozen qualified inputs, rules and versions** are replayed | Does not imply stable LLM outputs, sources, business decisions or target effects outside frozen evaluation |
| Decision cycle | Time from readiness freeze to decision readout, compared with customer baseline if documented | No invented reduction claims |
| Readiness and change | Evidence-backed blocker closure, re-evaluation after a relevant permission/policy/code change | Closure is only proven when fresh evidence qualifies; human release authority retained |
| Sandbox pilot product value | Workflow acceptance pass conditions, task quality, latency, unit cost, operator intervention, exceptions | These are prototype measurements separate from HAIEC's deterministic assurance conclusion; measure on representative declared test data |

Public headline metric for truthful proof:
"44 source-backed code-capable consequential action paths" in one sanitized Kestrel repository evaluation; recorded outcome REVIEW with authority, grant and observed evidence not established in that example. Link the actual /sample-reports/kestrel and /sample-reports. Do not infer 44 confirmed threats, 44 successful runtime exploits, percent coverage of enterprise estate, revenue protection or customer ROI. Homepage mentions other numbers; never combine denominators or benchmarks without checking artifact binding.

Value expression (NOT a fake interactive ROI calculator):
"Decision readiness = qualified evidence for the scoped claims + explicit material gaps + accountable next action."
If quantitative cost avoidance is used in sales, collect customer's baseline, probability model, exposure assumptions, remediation expense, expected time, owner validation and range sensitivity; never post speculative ROI or percent risk reduction.

## Distinctive value of deterministic HAIEC evaluation

Not "AI always gives deterministic answers." Rather:
- AI may explain, orchestrate authorized tools and guide sourcing;
- the canonical evaluation is rule/version based using admitted qualified evidence;
- the same frozen evidence/rules/version supports reproducible evaluation and digest comparison;
- the verdict is bounded by coverage, source relevance and unresolved unknowns;
- human executive authorization and target system enforcement remain separate.

This is the compelling buyer contrast: probabilistic assistant/agent actions versus deterministic, inspectable evaluation of qualified evidence. Avoid claiming all possible agent harms are deterministically detectable.

Suggested copy:
"The agent can propose an action. HAIEC evaluates the evidence with explicit rules. Your accountable owner decides whether to deploy."
"Same qualified inputs. Same rules. Inspectable evaluation."
"No green status manufactured from missing evidence."
