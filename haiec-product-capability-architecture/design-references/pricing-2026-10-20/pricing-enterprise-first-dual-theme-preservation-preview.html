Frequently asked questions

Understand your costs, limits and assurance boundary.

What's the difference between SCAN, DEFEND and ASSURE?

SCAN emphasizes source visibility; DEFEND adds qualified engineering and security checks where connected; ASSURE offers eligible deterministic assurance decisions and reports. Plan subscription does not guarantee every source, entitlement or target-native effect is ready.

Is the $999 plan intended only for audit firms?

No. ASSURE is for organizations evaluating their own systems. Firms serving clients should discuss FIRM, including client tenancy, reviewer workflows and professional rights.

What about Enterprise or Assurance Firms?

Enterprise POC is a separately scoped $25K–$75K typical engagement; FIRM is a custom practice agreement. Neither is an automatic self-service upgrade.

Does the 14-day SCAN trial require a payment card?

Current production routes disagree: checkout requires a card while signup promotes a no-card 14-day SCAN trial. The live behavior and entitlement lifecycle must be reconciled before publishing one definitive universal promise.

What are Pipelines and Executions?

Existing pricing defines a Pipeline as a compliance assessment workflow and an Execution as a run of that workflow. These are not necessarily the same as a canonical assurance evaluation or a runtime test vector. Backend metering must be verified.

What is runtime testing?

Authorized, bounded adversarial checks against supported target integrations. Runtime test availability does not mean a protection control has been installed in production.

What is MARPP?

A metadata-anchored, SHA-256 integrity-based evidence package where available. Hash integrity does not certify that upstream claims are true or that a target action was prevented.

Does Kill Switch SDK automatically stop agents?

No. It requires actual integration with the target execution point, permission to intervene and post-effect verification. An ASSURE subscription isn't automatically an effective runtime block.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Subscription changes must follow the tested billing terms and effective dates. Changes to plan access are separate from past reports and existing customer evidence.

Do you offer annual pricing?

The current source defines 20% annual savings for SCAN, DEFEND and ASSURE: $950.40, $3,350.40 and $9,590.40 per year respectively. Verify that live Stripe prices match before charging.

Does HAIEC conduct audits or certify compliance?

No. HAIEC supplies technical evidence and assurance tooling; qualified professional firms retain authority for applicable audit opinions, attestations and certifications.

Are reviewers, API keys, and clients counted as seats?

This is a proposed licensing definition to finalize, not an existing verified billing rule. Human builders/reviewers, programmatic keys and separate customer organizations should be governed independently under the contract.

Can I use one ASSURE subscription for multiple clients?

Do not assume so. Serving independent customer organizations, reselling client reports and branded delivery require firm-specific entitlement and legal terms.

What happens if HAIEC lacks evidence?

It reports the relevant unknown or not-assessed boundary. Buying a higher tier does not manufacture proof, grant authority or make an unsupported conclusion safe.

Will HAIEC run completely offline or locally?

Deployment, data handling and any offline mode must be technically qualified and contractually scoped; marketing claims alone do not establish an operational local distribution.
