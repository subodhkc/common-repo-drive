Telecom architecture and assurance questions

Specific proof, not unqualified autonomy claims.

What is the difference between telecom semantics and a complete telecom ontology?

HAIEC provides a 17-type read-model for selected network identities and a versioned operation-mapping pack. Both feed its common effect and assurance contracts. It does not include every SID entity, TM Forum API or vendor-specific network control implementation.

How does HAIEC treat ambiguous native telecom operations?

It preserves the original operation. A canonical verb is assigned only where a deterministic mapping exists. An ambiguous reboot or switchover may have a proposed mapping, but remains partial until an applicable business rule is established.

What role do TM Forum ODA, SID and Open APIs play?

They provide open telecom architectural and data interchange references. HAIEC can map qualified native evidence to its canonical assurance model where a supported adapter exists. Standards references do not establish conformance certification or live provider integration.

What can HAIEC examine for rApps and xApps?

Current HAIEC source includes rApp supply-chain, excessive-capability, lifecycle mismatch and A1 policy-integrity detection families. Actual findings depend on supported source visibility, applicable rules and evidence.

Is an AL2 scenario label proof of a security compromise?

No. AL2 is a declared state used to organize a specified forensic example. Claims about cause, attacker behavior or target-side consequence require their own qualified records.

What did the TM Forum 2026 event demonstrate?

The public archive documents a deterministic token-cap pass/breach pair, partial audit coverage, two C9 satisfied assessments, policy enforcement observations, an S2 false-certainty failure, an S3 safe escalation result and a monitored alert-delivery mechanism with declared boundaries.

Does the TM Forum report establish a competition win or organizer certification?

The source establishes a publisher-released, integrity-manifested workshop evidence package. A verified organizer placing, certification and byte-exact organizer upload receipt are separate claims not established by that archive alone.

Does a real telemetry sweep with no findings prove nothing went wrong?

No. In the workshop, 40 real spans were accepted with zero detector findings in the configured condition, while distinct control tests and forensic reconstruction found other issues. Sources and detection rules answer different questions.

Can HAIEC block any O-RAN or 5G network action?

Only configured, authorized and actually integrated execution points can mediate a target. A policy evaluation, source path finding or operator dashboard decision does not by itself establish network-side enforcement.

Can we start with one network AI system?

Yes. An Enterprise POC can focus on one authorized system and consequential question, such as a slice-policy change or network automation action. Exact source access and qualified adapter scope are agreed before an evaluation.
