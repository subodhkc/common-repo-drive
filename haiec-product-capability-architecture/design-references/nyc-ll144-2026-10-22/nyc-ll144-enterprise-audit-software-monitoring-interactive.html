05 / NYC LL144 obligations

Three duties. One connected evidence program.

The regulatory requirements belong to the employer or employment agency using a covered automated employment decision tool. HAIEC provides software and professional services for the applicable workflow.

01

Independent bias audit

A covered AEDT must be subjected to an independent bias audit within one year before its use, using the applicable selection/scoring and impact ratio calculations.

02

Advance candidate notice

Employers and agencies must provide the prescribed notice at least 10 business days before the covered AEDT is used, with required information and request procedures.

03

Public Summary of Results

Publish the required statistical summary and source/audit details through the employer's appropriate public employment website, subject to the rule's requirements.

Source: NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection. The required public Summary of Results and HAIEC's Public Evidence Reference are separate records.

09 / Questions before engagement

Answers for HR, counsel, audit firms and enterprise teams.

Practical distinctions between the annual independent audit, software operations, ongoing monitoring and public record verification.

How do audit availability requests work?

HAIEC scopes and pre-books professional bias-audit engagements. Your form requests a preferred timeframe, but it does not reserve a date. Availability and scope are confirmed after review.

Is a software calculation the independent LL144 audit?

No. The software supplies calculations, evidence and workflow tools. The formal independent bias audit is a separately reviewed professional engagement with its own findings and scope.

Can independent audit firms use HAIEC software?

Yes. Audit practitioners can request a tailored demonstration of statistical calculations, evidence packages, multi-engagement workflow support and artifact verification. Availability of particular firm-scale features is confirmed in the demo.

Does continuous monitoring replace the annual audit?

No. Monitoring helps track signals and evidence between reviews. It does not replace the LL144 requirement for a qualifying independent bias audit within one year before use.

What exactly does the public Artifact ID verify?

A HAIEC-issued evidence identifier can support checking the status and integrity of the issued artifact. It is not a NYC government license, proof that every source record was accurate, or a substitute for the employer's public Summary of Results.

Which hiring platforms can connect?

The product includes documented Greenhouse, Lever and Workday webhook adapters as well as generic webhook, API and CSV paths. Specific setup, data mapping and source permissions are scoped to your environment.

What if our candidate data is incomplete?

Data readiness is assessed with the engagement. Missing demographic groups, exclusions, source limitations and insufficient sample data should be recorded rather than hidden behind a favorable ratio.

What does deterministic mean in a bias audit?

Accepted fixed data, calculation configuration, analysis date and methodology version support reproducible scoped calculations and evidence digests. It does not mean the underlying hiring AI is deterministic or automatically fair.
