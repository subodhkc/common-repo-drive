Does a cataloged control mean it's active or effective?

No. Cataloged, implemented, evaluated, and verified in the relevant target are separate facts.

Does no finding observed mean passed?

No. It means the qualified bounded visibility scan did not observe a matching finding, not that the control was verified.

Are 88 and 45 additive control counts?

No. The former was the static framework-reference mapping, while the latter was the separately scoped assurance-control catalog at the audited source revision.

Does framework mapping certify compliance?

No. Mapping and evidence can help qualified reviewers, but they do not replace a framework owner's or independent auditor's determinations.
