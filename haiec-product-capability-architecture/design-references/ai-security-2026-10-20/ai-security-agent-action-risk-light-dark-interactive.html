Evidence quality

Know exactly what your security result establishes.

A security signal deserves a source, a scope, and a stated limit. Source findings and authorized runtime observations are independent. Missing evidence is not a clean result.

Source / Code-Capable

What implementation evidence shows may be possible

Source-level findings, rule identity, files, code patterns, scope, and supported paths to privileged actions. A code-capable path does not prove execution.

Runtime / Observed

What a selected and authorized test actually observed

Executed payloads, responses, safety gates, coverage and measured properties. Unexecuted tests do not pass, and a passing probe does not erase source risks.

Authority / Delegation

What policy, permissions and approved authority establish

When connected, relevant operator policy, effective grants and delegation evidence can be compared with a supported action path. Permission does not by itself authorize delegated discretion.

Control / Assurance

What can support a review and decision

Eligible evidence contributes to assurance questions, control findings and supported reports or receipts. Catalog entries, mappings and proposed tests are not proof of an operating safeguard.
