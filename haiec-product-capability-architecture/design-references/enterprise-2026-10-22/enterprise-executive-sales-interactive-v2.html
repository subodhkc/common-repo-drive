09 / Buyer questions

What your leadership and procurement team will ask.

Answers grounded in the scope of the engagement, not blanket platform guarantees.

What does HAIEC Assurance prove?

It evaluates specific claims about consequential AI actions against qualified evidence, explicit rules and recorded proof limits. An assessment supports a scoped decision, not a universal guarantee of safety.

What makes the evaluation deterministic?

HAIEC applies explicit evaluator logic to qualified, versioned evidence. Fixed accepted inputs and rules support repeatable scoped evaluations. AI assistants can help operate tools and explain results but do not author the canonical verdict.

Can we begin without connecting production?

Yes. An engagement can begin with declarations, architecture or approved source artifacts. Evidence beyond those sources remains unknown or unassessed unless separately qualified.

Can HAIEC build a prototype from our AI backlog?

The Assured AI Pilot Lab proposes a separately scoped sandbox build of one approved workflow, coupled with assurance review. It is not an unrestricted software development service or a substitute for production acceptance.

Is the Pilot Lab an independent audit?

No. If HAIEC contributes to the prototype build, its subsequent assessment is not represented as independent third-party verification. Independent review can be separately arranged when required.

What if evidence is incomplete?

Missing evidence remains explicit. The readout distinguishes established, partial, unknown and not-assessed claims and identifies the owner and next evidence needed.

How is an Assurance POC priced?

A typical existing-system POC is scoped at $25K–$75K with a commonly targeted 4–8 weeks after readiness. The agreed SOW controls delivery. The sandbox pilot has its own scoped feasibility and commercial terms.

Who makes the final launch decision?

The accountable organization does. HAIEC provides technical findings, an evidence-bounded evaluation and executive review options; it does not automatically approve production deployment or enforce changes in customer systems.
