Why consequence-level accountability matters

A valid tool call is not the whole answer.

An AI agent can use an approved tool and a valid credential while a consequential decision still raises questions about what shaped it, whose authority governed the choice and which effects are supported by evidence. ISAF v2 brings these questions into one research model.

Five independent evidence views

Separate what was requested, permitted, possible and observed.

Frequently asked questions

Understanding the ISAF v2 framework.

What is ISAF v2?

ISAF v2 is the Instruction Stack Audit Framework's evidence-bound research model for understanding what shaped AI behavior, how an agent action can cause effects, which authority governs the action and which claims the available evidence supports.

How is ISAF v2 different from the ISAF Logger?

The framework describes consequence-level accountability across instruction lineage, capability, delegated authority, evidence and time. The ISAF Logger is an earlier Python utility focused on generating selected training-lineage records. They play different roles.

What are the five evidence views?

Requested, Policy Authorized, Effectively Granted, Code Capable and Observed. Each answers an independent question and contributes facts only within its supported scope.

Why is delegated authority different from permission?

A credential may permit an API call. Delegated authority concerns who entrusted an agent to make a consequential business choice, such as selecting a payment recipient. An approved rule can sometimes determine the choice without additional discretion.

Can a technical risk be found before runtime logs are connected?

Yes. Supported code and source analysis can identify an action path and potential consequence. Runtime or target evidence is required for a claim that something actually occurred, not for every earlier source-level finding.

What is the Proof Frontier?

It is the exact boundary between what the current evidence supports and the next conclusion that would require a different or stronger source. Established findings remain useful even when another question is open.

What is the Evaluation Epoch?

It anchors the comparison to a compatible system, policy, grant, source and runtime state. This helps avoid mixing facts from unrelated or outdated configurations into one conclusion.

Is ISAF v2 an official regulatory certification?

No. ISAF v2 is an authored research framework. It can inform assurance work and standards mappings, but it does not itself issue regulatory certifications or guarantee safe production behavior.

Is the manuscript available on Zenodo?

The author-issued version 2.0 manuscript and print-ready edition have been prepared. Zenodo archival publication remains forthcoming. The official archive link will be added when a record and DOI are available.

How does HAIEC relate to ISAF v2?

HAIEC is a reference implementation of many evidence-bound concepts, including action-path analysis, distinct evidence views, delegated authority comparison and deterministic evaluation. Product availability and evidence coverage remain specific to the connected system and supported implementation.
