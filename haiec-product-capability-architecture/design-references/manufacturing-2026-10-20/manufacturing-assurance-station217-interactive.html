Manufacturing AI assurance explained

From source findings to decisions that matter.

Is HAIEC a manufacturing-only assurance platform?

No. Manufacturing is a domain reference application of HAIEC's general deterministic, evidence-bound platform. It does not introduce a separate manufacturing verdict engine.

Are the Station 217 HAIEC findings real?

The saved HAIEC evaluations are genuine canonical evaluator outputs over controlled manufacturing scenario evidence. They are not results from a live industrial customer or physical plant control system.

Did HAIEC block the originally incorrect work-order update?

No. The persisted evaluation identifies an earlier wrong-target event. The interactive digital factory separately demonstrates a later held action and human-review path. These are different events.

What does the procurement case show?

Financial approval and supplier qualification are independent. The factory scenario shows a purchase held when supplier qualification has expired, even where spending approval exists.

Is dashboard monitoring connected to live manufacturing telemetry?

The evidence replay uses recorded deterministic source data. Live continuous manufacturing monitoring is a separate integration and is not established by the demonstration dashboard.

How many manufacturing systems and cases are covered?

The source repository describes nine manufacturing AI systems and a current set of 45 persisted HAIEC evaluations across five scenario states. It also preserves an earlier 27-evaluation corpus and additional local demonstration fixtures.

Does a demonstration action record count as a HAIEC assurance receipt?

No. A browser demonstration record captures the local session's operating steps. Persisted HAIEC evaluations and their integrity and verification artifacts are distinct.

Can enterprises start without connecting every evidence source?

Yes. Supported implementation findings can identify relevant consequence paths. Policy, effective access, delegated authority and runtime observations strengthen the questions that require those facts.

How do I verify a specific assessment?

Use the public sample Master Report and its linked HAIEC verification receipt to inspect the exact reported evaluation scope and available integrity details.

Can we evaluate our own manufacturing AI system?

HAIEC can scope an enterprise evaluation around one consequential system and question, based on authorized available sources. Connected production controls or runtime effect witnesses require separate integration and validation.
