Discover: Reveal the consequential action path.

Find registered AI systems, agents, tools, source connections and code-capable relationships. Go beyond a list of endpoints to identify which downstream operations a supported implementation could reach.

The question
What can this AI system reach, call or change?
Relevant evidence
Authorized systems, connected source records, qualified code and supported tool-to-handler edges.
What this establishes
An inspectable action-capability lead, without claiming a registered component actually ran or caused an effect.

02 / Assure

Evaluate: Compare exact facts to defined rules.

Use canonical deterministic evaluation to compare relevant evidence about a proposed or recorded action with supported policy, technical capability, delegated authority and applicable controls.

The question
Does the evidence support the chosen action within this defined scope?
Relevant evidence
Qualified source facts, evaluation scope, operating rules, policy, identity and applicable control records.
What this establishes
Specific source-backed findings and an eligible bounded evaluation, not a universal safety verdict or inferred execution.

03 / Govern & Operate

Authorize: Put consequential choices under accountable authority.

Determine whether approved policy already resolves a protected choice or an exception requires bounded delegation or a decision from the appropriate human owner.

The question
Who may approve this target, amount, recipient or operating exception?
Relevant evidence
Operating policy, trusted task facts, explicit role/delegation and configured approval workflow.
What this establishes
A scoped authorization or review decision where configured, not proof that a provider credential was granted or a target-side action blocked.

04 / Govern & Operate

Monitor: Investigate change in connected operating sources.

Inspect qualified logs, traces, source health and other collected runtime facts to compare known operating states and investigate changes where customers have configured supported instrumentation.

The question
What changed in the AI system and which connected source recorded it?
Relevant evidence
Authorized evidence binders, structured or OTLP ingestion, relevant baselines and source freshness.
What this establishes
A bounded change or observation in connected sources. Configuration does not prove signal receipt; receipt does not prove evaluation.

05 / Govern & Operate

Protect: Support action controls at the actual execution boundary.

Apply configured guards and qualified operator interventions to supported customer applications and gateway points. Establish which routes are actually mediated before making an enforcement claim.

The question
Can this action be held, denied, stopped or recovered by a control actually connected to the target?
Relevant evidence
Installed guard or execution adapter, authorized operator, target binding, control operation and native effect readback.
What this establishes
Supported controlled operations and their specific evidence where integrated, never universal protection from a dashboard decision.

06 / Assure

Verify: Make the evaluated result independently inspectable.

Review saved HAIEC evaluations with their exact source and scope, Agent Audits, reports and issued verification artifacts. Keep an inspectable chain between a supported finding and what a reviewer may claim later.

The question
Which result was issued, and what exact evidence and limitations support it?
Relevant evidence
Persisted evaluation identity, digest-bound reports, qualified source references and an issued receipt where available.
What this establishes
Result and artifact integrity within the specific evaluated scope, not independent certification or proof that unconnected source data is true.

Questions about HAIEC capabilities

One platform, distinct and accountable jobs.

Do I need to run the six capabilities in this order?

No. They are independently selectable customer goals, not a required six-stage onboarding journey. Each still depends on relevant source availability and permissions.

Are the six capabilities separate packages or subscriptions?

No. They describe HAIEC's platform assurance jobs. Independent developer security packages such as AI AppSec, MCP Tenant Isolation and LLMVerify have their own scope and product pages.

Why is DAI not the seventh capability?

Delegated Action Integrity compares consequential choice authority with applicable facts across relevant evidence. It is cross-cutting reasoning for assessment and authorization, rather than a peer job family.

Can Discover show useful risk before monitoring is enabled?

Yes. Qualified source analysis can identify an action-capable route and its technical consequence. It does not claim the action executed or establish unsupported authority facts.

Does Authorize create an IAM permission or automatically block a target call?

No. A recorded human or policy decision differs from effective IAM grants and an installed enforcement point. These have separate owners and evidence requirements.

Is Monitor always on for every system?

No. Qualified source binders, signal intake and relevant comparison coverage must be present. A configured collector is not proof of evaluated telemetry or a known production effect.

Does Protect mean HAIEC can stop any AI agent anywhere?

No. Action protection requires compatible customer-side execution controls, correct identity and authorization, actual interception and effect readback. A control listing or evaluation does not guarantee protection.

Does Verify certify compliance or validate that every source claim is true?

No. It supports inspection of exact issued reports, evidence scope and artifact integrity. Independent certification and native source truth have their own obligations.

Can a connected AI assistant create the canonical HAIEC verdict?

No. The assistant can guide and operate eligible operations. HAIEC's recorded deterministic evaluator remains the source of the canonical result.
