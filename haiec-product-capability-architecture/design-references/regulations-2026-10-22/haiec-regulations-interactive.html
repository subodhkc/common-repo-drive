05 / HAIEC EVIDENCE MODEL

Move from a regulatory question to technical facts.

HAIEC examines consequential AI through independent evidence views. A regulatory obligation is not automatically satisfied by scanning code, running a test or issuing a receipt.

INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE VIEWS

Five distinct questions. One bounded assurance foundation.

What can your AI request, what is authorized, what permissions exist, what can the code enable and what was actually observed?

RequestedWhat action or authority is sought.
Policy AuthorizedWhat applicable policy permits.
Effectively GrantedWhat permissions and grants enable.
Code CapableWhat source evidence supports as technically possible.
ObservedWhat qualified tests and connected records actually show.

These are independent evidence views, not five mandatory sequential steps.
