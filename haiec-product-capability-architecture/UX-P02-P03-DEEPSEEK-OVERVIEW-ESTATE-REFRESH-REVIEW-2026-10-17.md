## C. Reject specific semantic/data claims and behaviors in new mock

1. `ladderHtml(count)` uses `i<=count`: zero still paints one dot. The 7-rung universal `FILE_STORED` → `ASSURANCE_EVALUATED` ladder is **not** HAIEC's five action evidence planes, detector coverage or lifecycle. Never adopt.
2. `Fully qualified` count is `ladder===5 && attention===0`; source code plainly fabricates a maturity verdict. “Clean” is also inferred solely from no listed attention. Neither condition establishes complete assurance, protection or unknown-free inventory.
3. The per-system five-plane arrays are synthetic blanket system statuses, not qualified action-level evidence; `EFFECTIVELY_GRANTED` cannot be marked green from partial IAM/declared envelope.
4. The mock labels a cross-system edge `Verified delegation` without the issuer grant + target independently qualified evidence needed by cross-system DAI; `observed only` does not itself prove authority or target effect.
5. Hero `11 attention items / 3 decisions`, 24-hour `+3/-2`, source health `4/1/2`, per-provider usage `$28.40`, “attributed systems 4 of 7,” `12 controls` all originate in hardcoded fixtures, **not** canonical HAIEC measurements; do not port them or promote them as real.
6. “Recent evaluations” rows call `selectSystem` and **never select their exact historical evaluation IDs**; receipt/exact-evaluation binding and frozen as-of must be preserved at P04/P07/P11.
7. A policy approval record and deployment-review record share the sample DECISIONS array. Real E6.2 My Work does **not** prove generic policy-approval queue parity. Never imply that human decision authorizes dispatch, PEP or target readback.
8. Action controls rely on `onclick`/string identifiers and largely `toast` previews, not native auth/permissions. Client-side role switcher is a presentation demo; unsafe to treat as authorization.
9. Search, diagram, and responsive hidden-column failures are **mock-only defects**, not bugs reproduced in HAIEC production. No reason to replace shipped P02/P03 to achieve visual parity.
