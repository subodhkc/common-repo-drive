## Why this enhancement
The existing page gave technically sound explanations, but executives could not see quickly what decisions or measurable operational value insurance AI assurance would unlock. Non-payment examples in the HTML used synthetic dollar thresholds where domain-specific authority checkpoints would be more credible.

## Implemented — insurance landing
- Preserve the original consequence-first hero and improve the USP: model testing examines outputs, access auditing examines credentials, and HAIEC connects consequential decision rights, technical reachability, effective permissions and qualified evidence.
- Primary CTA: insurance assurance POC; optional demo in new tab.
- Four executive value families: transformation (decision lead time), operations (bounded automation coverage), risk/audit (evidence sufficiency and review friction), CIO/security (excess grants and tool reachability). **KPIs are proposed pilot metrics; no invented results or ROI.**
- Concrete action scenarios, deployment release evidence, CSM6/DAI/ISAF, governance and standards as scoped support rather than certification.
- Link real *cross-domain* proof references to the manufacturing Station 217 evaluation and bounded telecom workshop findings, without implying any insurance-specific canonical run.
- Show business owner / model or specialist risk review / HAIEC technical evidence / deployment approval / operational monitoring and audit as complementary governance roles.
- Six tangible initial POC artifact categories: action and consequence map; authority matrix; evidence and control coverage; prioritized gap register; source-qualified technical findings; executive decision readout. Artifacts depend on agreed eligible scope.
- Commercial terms aligned with canonical HAIEC `ENTERPRISE_POC`: $25K–$75K commonly 4–8 weeks after source readiness. A shorter accelerated pilot is only a contingent option. Scope and SOW govern.
- AI-risk and Insurance Evidence Passport proposals clearly distinguished from existing enterprise assurance, licensed insurer underwriting and actual certification.
