## Addendum — Accept Synthetic Human Governance and Execution Controls in HAIEC Demonstrations

**Reviewed after Sprint 24, 2026-10-16.** The earlier PDF validation used an unnecessarily restrictive creative-use standard. HAIEC's manufacturing experience **already contains fictional scoped approval, rejection and pending requests; reviewer-role evidence; email/text previews; fixed-clock monitoring sources; and deterministic synchronized playback of held/allowed/blocked operations**. These should be proudly presented as an **operating-model demonstration of HAIEC**, not removed.

- **Actual HAIEC engine on synthetic evidence:** Station 217's Oct 14 source input includes a previously recorded target-divergent transaction. The deterministic detection identifies it. No inference of factory impact or prevention.
- **Fictional governance:** Station 217's subsequent expanded MES write request is PENDING with a Quality Director role assigned; lower-risk advisory work remains in declared scope.
- **Simulation gateway demonstration:** A *later, separate* request to enlarge MES writes is rejected/held in the virtual playback. It is accurate to write "the demo held the attempted expansion" or "a simulated control prevents the subsequent demo write." It is inaccurate to assert that HAIEC stopped the *earlier already recorded* synthetic transaction or physically controlled a live factory.
- **Fictional readiness gate:** "PASSED" values for fabricated org entitlement, system registration or reviewer eligibility are allowed as **source-authored demo states** only after a matching versioned fixture field exists. They cannot be represented as tested live product entitlements or authenticated employee signatures.
- **Monitoring:** stale `torque-tool-event-log` and missing `mes-release-readback` in the Oct 15 fixed-clock scenario are compatible with a qualified target readback in the **different Oct 14 evaluated transaction**. Keep event and transaction identities separate.
- **Product UX:** No repeated "synthetic" badges across each block. Put a small "Demo playback" marker by player controls and the full methodology on the last dedicated page. Showcase HAIEC's three pillars and six independently invocable user goals up front.

Authoritative implementation guidance and page-by-page corrections live in [MAA product story](https://github.com/subodhkc/manufacturing-ai-assurance-haiec/tree/main/docs/product-story), particularly `SYNTHETIC-CONTROL-DEMO-ACCEPTANCE-AND-S25-STORYBOARD.md`. **No new engine/evaluation rerun is warranted to tell the full product story.**

## Product decision

MAA is a **manufacturing reference implementation / demonstrator of HAIEC**, not an independently forked security product. All manufacturing dashboard work should lead with **HAIEC's enterprise USP** and demonstrate relevant capabilities throughout the product, using nine manufacturing AI systems as representative operating examples.

Hero: **Deploy AI with confidence. Stay in control of every consequential action.**

Keep the three primary pillars from the reviewed product plan:

- **Understand** — system/source discovery, capabilities, reach and action consequences.
- **Assure** — evidence qualification, deterministic evaluation, delegated action integrity, applicable controls and bounded findings.
- **Govern & Operate** — operating policy, accountable decisions, monitoring, protection only where integrated, change investigation and verification.

Expose **Discover · Evaluate · Authorize · Monitor · Protect · Verify** as **six independently available goals**. These are **not one mandatory six-step setup pipeline** and do not mean all prerequisites must be true for all goals.

## Nonnegotiable presentation contract

1. **Largest area:** HAIEC USP and what customers accomplish; show available capability and business value before risk details and unavailable-integration warnings.
2. **Middle:** guided case study of enterprise AI system → potential consequence → qualified evidence/assurance → operating scope → human reviewer → activity/monitoring illustration → verified report. Allow visitor to select goals and systems out of sequence.
3. **Bottom, once:** "About this demonstration" page: actual HAIEC engines evaluated synthetic manufacturing input and saved 45 current, 27 historical assessments; nine fictional human reviewer decisions, staged email/SMS, scenario telemetry, scope-aware control illustrations and local containment actions are not real industrial customer integrations.
4. **Locally at animation:** small "Demo playback" marker plus correct source classification whenever needed to prevent a visualized event being mistaken for real action/delivered message. Do not repeat the full methodology after every card.
5. **Technical accuracy:** MAA cannot claim verified live MES/PLC enforcement, independent physical readback, all five action authority dimensions assessed, actual customer reviewer authentication of fictional dossier personas, or guaranteed machine safety.
6. **Retain engine truth:** all 45 current overall assessments stay Review required. Station 217's qualified fictional target mismatch is real HAIEC output on synthetic evidence; its human MES-write expansion decision is separately fictional/pending.
7. **No duplicated infrastructure:** Source project owner remains `subodhkc/haiec-website`. MAA is static-read and scenario presentation, and must not build a second engine, audit ledger or approval source of truth.

## Alignment with existing common-repo decisions

- The shared capability matrix and diagrams are **planning/provenance**, not executed test results.
- Orthogonal readiness/entitlement/permissions/integration/evidence/human-approval checks are **per-goal prereqs**, not a universal seven-level pass/fail assembly line.
- The 87-row matrix is audited but **not exhaustive**. Do not imply all possible capabilities or external integrations are complete.
- `PRODUCT-TRUTH-RELEASE-GATE-STATUS.json` currently records [HAIEC PR #2220](https://github.com/subodhkc/haiec-website/pull/2220) **OPEN_READY_FOR_REVIEW_NOT_MERGED** and with tests not run. Do not claim its provider scanner quarantine, control hardening or production smoke as deployed in Main until verified.
- "Observed" from an authenticated telemetry receipt does not necessarily mean a native action/effect was established. "Monitor" and "Protect" independently need configured integration and source/effect witness readiness.
- No new five-plane requirements should be forced onto a static source assessment when evidence/source workflow doesn't need them.
