## Product positioning

**H1:** Know when manufacturing AI reaches the wrong action.
**Deck:** An AI agent can have valid access and still update the wrong work order, release the wrong inventory or approve a purchase commitment outside its business authority. HAIEC connects source and action evidence to the policy, delegated choice and actual effect that matter.

**Not a separate manufacturing engine.** The manufacturing dashboard is an industry reference experience for HAIEC's Understand, Assure, Govern & Operate platform. The underlying HAIEC deterministic evaluator is canonical. The dashboard itself replays and visualizes synthetic manufacturing operations alongside genuine persisted HAIEC evaluations of those synthetic sources.

## Exactly what is demonstrated

**Nine AI systems, sixteen agent roles, seven manufacturing workflows**, a 45-entry control catalog, twelve scenario definitions, 27 three-state test fixtures and 39 scenario cases are described in repository README. There are **27 genuine persisted v1 HAIEC evaluations (9x3)** plus **45 genuine persisted v3 HAIEC evaluations (9x5)** over synthetic manufacturing evidence. The current 45 case export set and source-world independence matter more than a generic scanner demo. The dashboard uses a read-only adapter to validate exact payload hashes and project findings without recomputing canonical verdicts. The previous 27 cases are a historical separate corpus, not additive live scanned customers.

**Station 217 is the flagship.** Approved target WO-217-4802, recorded target WO-217-4803. Canonical HAIEC rule MFG-EFF-001 supports the wrong-target divergence and REVIEW disposition in the persisted evaluation. A **later, separate** digital-factory attempt is held in the local demonstration gateway and routed for human Quality Director review; advisory operations may continue. Do not claim HAIEC retroactively blocked the earlier event. Code-level simulator boundary is isolated from plant I/O, and client interactions do not drive real MES/PLC devices.

**ERP procurement:** Financial approval and supplier qualification remain independent gates. A financially authorized purchase can still be held if the supplier qualification expired; a separately qualified case proceeds in the synthetic digital factory. This is a policy/operation demonstration, not a real enterprise ERP integration.

**Controls, human reviews and monitoring:** The dashboard shows multiple operations, role-scoped decision requests, held versus allowed actions, anomaly and signal-loss events and an exported browser demonstration record. The record is a synthetic local browser-session artifact, **not a HAIEC-issued canonical evaluation or real-time plant event**. Source-backed evidence replay is **Mode A**, a 60s deterministic playback; **Mode B live monitoring is deferred**. Do not sell universal production protection, human notification delivery, or live telemetry based only on this dashboard.

**Extra source truth:** docs/hackfw/FINAL-PACKAGE.md lists the final 113.8s narrated video in repo docs/hackfw/screens-v29/judge-path-final-v29.mp4, companion captions, judge path, reports, and unfinished external video hosting.
