## 3. P1 evidence-integrity risk: `EVALUATION_BOUND` is not necessarily an exact historical DAI input snapshot

**Source-established mechanics:**
1. `lib/mcp/server.ts` accepts optional `assuranceEvaluationId` after org/system match but does **not itself require `evaluationStatus:'COMPLETED'`**. In the assembler the capability-binding query is completed-only, so a pending/failed chosen ID may still allow *current approved envelope* or delegation facts and then return `found:true` / `basis:'EVALUATION_BOUND'`.
2. `lib/assurance/dai-assembler.ts` when `input.evaluationId` is supplied captures `evaluation.evaluationSnapshotAt`, limits observed telemetry producer runs to that evaluation's `audit_run_evidence_attachments`, limits *rule confirmations* by `confirmedAt <= boundEvaluationSnapshot` and limits capability bindings to the exact completed eval. **But the operating-envelope query filters only `organizationId,state:'APPROVED',aiSystemId` and takes the latest by `createdAt`**; it does not select the historically approved envelope as of the snapshot. The **delegation evidence query also selects latest rows with no `collectedAt <= evaluationSnapshotAt` cutoff**, and its merged dimension facts may include after-snapshot rows. `asOf` takes the maximum timestamp across those families, so an EVALUATION_BOUND report can show asOf **later** than its chosen snapshot. Rule cutoff alone is insufficient.
3. Thus **later policy or delegation facts can alter a historical U5-bound DAI result** although its capability facts and some telemetry/rules are bound. A successful identity check **does not imply time-basis integrity**. This is not an established cross-tenant leak or reproduced live historic-data failure: the risk is **code-level and testable** with pre/post-snapshot fixture rows.

**Remediation requirement:** Prefer a bounded **P05 MCP evidence-integrity correction** *before expanding this historical-bound tool workflow*. First inventory persisted source versioning and whether a truly historically authorized/approved envelope version and delegation evidence can be reconstructed. Require `COMPLETED` for optional U5 snapshot when claiming Evaluation-bound truth. For each participating source family enforce historical eligibility/provenance and exact evaluation-scope join; where historical envelope/version cannot be established, **do not pretend frozen**: withhold that plane or return explicit `MIXED_TIME_BASIS`/`HISTORICAL_POLICY_NOT_ESTABLISHED` limitations and label entire result as *partial/mixed-time contextual investigation* (not frozen Evaluation fact), rather than recomputing historical delegated grants from today's envelope. Keep preserved P05 current-assembled UI and current tool path unchanged. Do not retroactively mutate reports or change U5 verdict; no new DAI engine.

**Behavioral test plan:** (i) two completed U5 evaluations before/after a policy/grant update, (ii) post-snapshot APPROVED envelope must **not** appear as bound-eval authorization, (iii) post-snapshot delegation evidence must not be unioned into the historic delegated plane, (iv) rule cutoff and run-scoped telemetry remain intact, (v) pending/failed U5 `assuranceEvaluationId` cannot be labeled `EVALUATION_BOUND`, (vi) current-assembled still sees latest qualified facts, (vii) org/system mismatch remains non-enumerating 404 and scope is `report:read`, (viii) missing historical policy/version surfaces UNKNOWN/NOT_ASSESSED rather than MADE_UP/HISTORICALLY_AUTHORIZED, and (ix) test target + UI/MCP identity/time-basis parity. Avoid new broad replay architecture.
