## 2. Product truth: cannot be an upsell
**Lead promise:** "HAIEC's deterministic assurance engine evaluates what qualified evidence establishes about AI capability, authority, control coverage and consequences. Results state the applicable basis and what remains unknown. Optional deeper validation, continuous operational integrations and target-native controls can extend that evidence and operating scope; they are not prerequisites for the baseline analysis."

**Never charge for fixing a defect or hide essential results behind an operational extension:**
- accurate qualified findings, exact evaluation identity, evidence/provenance and explicit unknowns;
- source health/freshness and honest E4/P10 labels; inapplicability vs NOT_ASSESSED;
- correct tenant/role/entitlement boundaries, human approval integrity and fail-safe behavior;
- canonical report/evidence authenticity *where the underlying product feature is entitled*;
- honest one-best-next-action guidance and disclosure of the selected workflow's minimum inputs.
We cannot infer universal account entitlement or plan inclusion from code existence. Say **product-native when available under customer's existing plan and permissions**, not "free for every tenant".

The customer is not buying a better verdict engine. They are buying **more qualified evidence, greater exercised scope, real customer integration, longer-running operational service, or reviewed evidence delivery**.

## 3. Ownership classification, orthogonal to price
**PRODUCT_NATIVE** = canonical HAIEC compute/read/report outcome under actual supported scope and entitlement.
**CUSTOMER_OWNED** = client supplies policy, owner, credential consent, review, response and target resources; customer declaration is not independent technical proof.
**OPTIONAL_EXTENSION** = separately scoped integration, testing, recurring service or manual operating assistance that adds a measurable outcome; never a substitute for product-native correctness.

For each of the 76 existing IDs, require a reconciliation row: activity ID; actual code/route/registered MCP or NOT_AVAILABLE; source & entitlement; above ownership class(es); outcome claim and boundaries; proposed family; pilot evidence; status. Do not assign a paid label or new code for all IDs merely because they exist in a catalog.

## 6. Generic self-service template: research, source model and rollout
The model is **one modular optional client worksheet**, not mandatory HAIEC input, and not five new copies of your assurance output. [Template v0.1](templates/CLIENT-OPERATIONS-EXTENSION-WORKSHEET-v0.1.md).

**Research to reuse rather than invent:** [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/) offers voluntary actions and JSON/CSV/Excel; never use as forced ordered checklist. [NIST GenAI Profile](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) optional profile. [CSA AICM v1.1](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/ai-controls-matrix-v1-1) published June 22, 2026, with 247 objectives in 18 domains and accompanying AI assessment resources; map as external namespace, **not** 247 HAIEC controls. Check licenses before copying questionnaires. [ISO/IEC 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) management-system lifecycle and continuous improvement; **do not reproduce copyright clauses** or imply certification. Reuse OTel/CloudEvents for qualified event transport and customers' existing gateways/IAM/ITSM rather than parallel engines.

**Universal record fields:** client objective; system/environment; exact current HAIEC result/snapshot and evidence; source qualification/unknown; selected optional operating goal; client-vs-HAIEC responsibilities; only needed extra source and consent; optional test and safe scope; desired measurable improvement; target-side proof; failure and data minimization; named reviewer/response owner; review/renewal trigger; canonical next action. Additional operator or regulator detail is optional. Versions always distinguish draft/client declaration vs provider qualified vs HAIEC evaluated.

**Template variants from ONE source:** Quickstart (nontechnical), Engineer (tool/PEP/telemetry), Operator (signal/incident/recovery), Governance (owner/control/applicability), Executive (scope/metric/evidence). Not separate competing plan engines or persistence enums.
