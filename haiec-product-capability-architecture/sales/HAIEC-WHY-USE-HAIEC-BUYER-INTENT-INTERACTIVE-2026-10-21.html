04 / Why HAIEC

More tools are not always the missing answer.
A defensible decision is.

Security scanning, identity systems, policy workflows and monitoring each contribute valuable facts. The assurance challenge is understanding those facts together without overstating what any one of them proves.

01Security tests

Find source vulnerabilities, risky patterns and tested behaviors.

Question: What was found or exercised?
02IAM and permissions

Show what a specific authenticated identity is technically granted.

Question: Who can invoke which resource?
03Policy and governance

Define approved intent, responsibilities and decision limits.

Question: What should be allowed?
04Runtime observations

Show activity observed by qualified, bounded telemetry sources.

Question: What was seen within coverage?
The practical difference: HAIEC connects available evidence to consequential action and authority questions. It can surface meaningful source-backed findings early while explicitly recording what still requires policy, IAM, telemetry or target-side verification.
