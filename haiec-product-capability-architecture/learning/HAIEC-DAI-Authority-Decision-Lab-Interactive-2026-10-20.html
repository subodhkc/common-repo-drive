FOUNDATION / 02

Four questions behind one powerful credential.

All four can describe the same AI agent. None automatically answers the others.

01 / REQUEST

What was asked?

Customer asks for a refund. A request does not authorize an arbitrary bank-account choice.

02 / POLICY

What is allowed?

Standing rules permit specified refunds and limits, not every discretionary outcome.

03 / GRANT

What can credentials do?

The provider API may accept any account ID. This describes access, not business decision authority.

04 / DELEGATED CHOICE

Who may decide?

Trusted rules may fix an outcome, or an authorized owner must delegate the remaining protected choice.

THE METHOD / 03

Five views of reality. No shortcut between them.

The five evidence views are peers. DAI compares authority relationships across available facts; it is not a sixth evidence plane.

EVIDENCE LENS 01 / 05
