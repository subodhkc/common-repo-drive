## Built experience

1. Premium light-theme executive hero with animated illustrative agent → tool → sensitive business resource, explicit difference between reachable source code and performed effect.
2. Four peer executive questions: actual assessment proof, accountable human operating decision, customer-native enforcement/target witness, and residual-risk owner.
3. Three authored educational cases: (a) supplier bank-detail changes from a source-capable path, (b) vendor AI customer CRM access despite general vendor assurance, (c) stale release/policy approval and provider event without qualified target witness.
4. Twelve independent three-option decision checks, one per case gate across interpretation, minimum evidence, scoped decision and ownership. Correct-answer source limits and review feedback are authored, not generative.
5. Three learning modes: Executive Brief (four core choices), Full Workshop (twelve) and Facilitator (added discussion prompts). Four-step progress tracked in memory, no account/login or persistent storage.
6. Five individually inspectable evidence planes per case: Requested, Policy Authorized, Effectively Granted, Code Capable, Observed. No implied complete integration or cross-plane proof promotion.
7. Six evidence-change counterfactuals showing precisely what a new policy, principal grant, target block witness, release change, expired human approval or runtime log can and cannot establish.
8. Four risk-treatment choices and their required conditions: narrowly scoped pilot, minimum missing evidence, remediate/retest, or stop/no expansion.
9. Source-grounded public Kestrel proof panel: 44 code-capable consequential paths and a REVIEW result, without presenting 44 findings as incidents, effective IAM, observed runtime effect or actual customer protection.
10. Executive AI Deployment Decision Brief with system/operation question, sponsor, risk owner, separate reviewer, draft risk treatment, allowed/excluded operations, qualified facts, missing source, stop/expiry/review terms and notes.
11. Explicit *Use this scenario in my decision brief* action. Switching cases does not silently mix two cases' memo facts. Educational sample facts are clearly labeled and must be replaced with actual customer sources.
12. Self-contained no-network runtime, accessible labels/controls, local Markdown/JSON downloads, printer-friendly all-three-case/twelve-question appendix and optional browser print-to-PDF.

## HAIEC source contract alignment

Reviewed against current default branch sources:
- `lib/assurance/deployment-decision.ts`: PENDING, APPROVED_SCOPED, APPROVED_CONDITIONAL, REJECTED, REVOKED, SUPERSEDED; stale/expired approval read model; no requester self-approval.
- `lib/assurance/types.ts`: U5 ALLOW/REVIEW/BLOCK are scoped assurance dispositions, not operating decisions or native guards.
- `lib/assurance/enforcement-read-model.ts`: configuration, authorized scope, dispatch and provider acknowledgement are not equivalent to target-applied or independently effect-verified protection.
- `lib/assurance/assurance-question-projection.ts`: five separate evidence planes and actual proof frontier.
- `lib/assurance/master-report-product.ts`: one persisted frozen evaluation, not automatically the current system state.
- `lib/assurance/public-verification-display.ts`: receipt integrity is not product safety or a human operating decision.
- `app/enterprise/data.ts`: commercial outcomes differ from U5 dispositions and canonical deployment decision states.

This training page does not recompute a HAIEC assurance result, submit a deployment approval, connect a customer system or manufacture missing evidence. Public Kestrel facts are reference only. Other cases are fictional instructional material.
