Stage 05 · Response

Findings, acknowledgment, and approval have different owners

An alert can be delivered before anyone acknowledges it. An owner can acknowledge an incident without authorizing a production operation.

Critical distinction

Notification attempted is not provider acceptance. Provider acceptance is not delivery confirmation. Delivery confirmation is not authenticated human acknowledgment. Acknowledgment is not an approval.

Human operating decisions

In real HAIEC, decision records are scoped, versioned, role-authorized and may expire or be revoked. This workbook performs no approval writes.
