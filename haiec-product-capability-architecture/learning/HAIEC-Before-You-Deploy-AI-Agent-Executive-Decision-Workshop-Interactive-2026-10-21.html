01 / THE DECISION FRAMEWORK

Four questions.
Four different kinds of proof.

A security finding is not a deployment approval. An approved operation is not automatically protected at runtime. And neither removes the need for an accountable decision about residual business risk.

ASSURANCE RESULT
HAIEC's ALLOW, REVIEW or BLOCK is a conclusion within the evaluated scope. It does not itself authorize production deployment or prove customer-native interception.
