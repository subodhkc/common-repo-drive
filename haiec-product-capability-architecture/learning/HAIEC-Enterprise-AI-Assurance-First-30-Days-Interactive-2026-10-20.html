YOUR LEARNING PATH

Four weeks. One defensible decision.

Each week produces a concrete artifact or a clearly qualified evidence gap. You can enter at any point that fits your authorized goal.

FIRST PRINCIPLES

What you are actually establishing

UNDERSTAND

See the consequence

Map the AI system, principals, tools, data and possible action-to-business-effect paths, using source-qualified relationships.

ASSURE

Compare the evidence

Test supported controls and compare request, policy, effective grants, code reachability and observation independently.

GOVERN & OPERATE

Act with accountability

Assign real human decisions, monitor what sources can show, connect native controls where needed and verify the report trail.

Designed for two entry modes. Explore using public example reports, or work in your organization with genuine role, source, and evaluation permissions. Completing a lesson is not an HAIEC evaluation or product approval. The six capabilities can be used independently and the 30-day sequence is not a prerequisite checklist.
ONE RECURRING SCENARIO

A purchasing agent with more reach than intended

The example teaches what code, credentials, policy, discretionary choice and observation each reveal. Specific numbers below are illustrative teaching values, not HAIEC findings.

Business requestProcess a permitted purchase under policy limits
Agent + toolInvoke procurement tool through a callable handler
Effective accessService credential may reach broader write API
Target outcomePurchase record changes only if dispatched and applied

A reachable write path is useful security evidence even if no runtime event is present. Approval, effective grant, delegation, dispatch and independently witnessed effect each require their own support.

METHOD · ISAF V2 · DAI

An AI action has several different truths

Authorization and ability can diverge. ISAF v2 supplies a research/methodological framework. HAIEC's shipping DAI engine has its own bounded implemented comparator and should not be presented as every proposed ISAF v2 capability.

A source can establish a strong code-path finding before all five planes are populated. Treat each evidence plane as an independent question, not a required integration step or substitute for another plane.
01Requested

What operation was asked for?

Evidence: Prompt, request, ticket or tool inputA request is not approval.
02Policy Authorized

What explicit policy permits?

Evidence: Approved business/operating envelopePolicy does not imply current IAM grant.
03Effectively Granted

What privileges are available?

Evidence: Provider-issued IAM/principal evidenceIAM does not prove the agent used the grant.
04Code Capable

What could implementation reach?

Evidence: Source/tool/call graphReachability is not execution.
05Observed

What is witnessed in scope?

Evidence: Qualified runtime/action telemetryA trace does not prove downstream business effect alone.
DELEGATED ACTION INTEGRITY

Nine dimensions of a consequential choice

A credential may enable a broad operation. Did approved policy fix the choice, or did an agent exercise independent discretion needing qualified delegation evidence? The answer depends on exact sources, scope and time.

01OperationWhich action or API operation?
02TargetWhich resource, service or record?
03ScopeWhich objects, accounts or tenants?
04MagnitudeWhat amount, count or scale?
05DurationHow long is the grant or action valid?
06ApprovalWhich qualified human or policy authorization?
07Data classWhat sensitivity/classification?
08EnvironmentDevelopment, test or production?
09GeographyWhich region or jurisdiction?

Policy vs grant vs delegated choice

A written $500 refund limit is relevant policy evidence. A $10,000 API permission is a separate grant. A reachable refund handler establishes code capability. Whether an agent chose an amount under qualified discretionary authority is a further question. A tool response alone does not verify that funds moved.

Missing delegated authority evidence is not itself proof of an unauthorized delegated action. Preserve valid policy and code findings while making unresolved facets explicit.

Evidence / effect frontier

At each claimed link, ask: Who asserted it? Which actor and system? Which environment? What time? Is the source identity qualified? Was action merely requested, enabled by code, sent, observed or independently read back at the target?

Cross-system delegation and target proof must be labeled unverified where the required per-hop identity, issuer and correlation facts are not established.

IMPLEMENTED DAI STATUS VOCABULARY

Seven bounded DAI result states

INSIDE_ESTABLISHED_DELEGATIONSupported facts are within a qualified delegated scope.
CAPABILITY_OUTSIDE_DELEGATION_MEDIATEDA broader reachable capability exists, with source-backed mediation evidence.
CAPABILITY_OUTSIDE_DELEGATION_UNMEDIATEDA broader reachable capability exists without the required established mediation.
GRANT_BROADER_THAN_DELEGATIONProvider grant exceeds established delegated scope.
OBSERVED_OUTSIDE_DELEGATIONQualified observed action exceeds established delegation.
UNRESOLVED_DELEGATION_EXPOSURESome relevant delegated comparison remains unresolved.
NOT_ASSESSEDRequired delegation evidence is absent for assessment.

MONITOR · PROTECT · INCIDENT RESPONSE

Know what is observed, operating and enforced

HAIEC separates monitoring into four independently supported lanes. A result in one lane does not automatically establish another. Use these views to design a practical on-call operating loop.

LENS 01

Passive telemetry and source health

Are qualified sources delivering now?

Check declared cadence, last accepted qualified receipt, identity, freshness, quarantine, retention, coverage and a responsible source owner.

Do not infer: Configured ≠ delivering; stale history is not current coverage.

Owner view: Sources and Telemetry
LENS 02

Authorized tests and detections

What actually ran, and with what coverage?

Check test authorization, category selectability, execution, analyzer identity, finding basis, NOT_RUN/HELD, related vulnerability hypotheses and source-vs-runtime distinction.

Do not infer: Cataloged tests and rule registrations are not executed findings.

Owner view: Runtime Tests and Risks
LENS 03

Approved policy and decision state

Who authorized the envelope or response?

Check approval/version, human decision actor, DAI contradictions, policy change, scope limits, alert case and human ACK.

Do not infer: Policy configured or notification delivered is not target enforcement or acknowledged response.

Owner view: Guardrails and My Work
LENS 04

Native target enforcement and effect

What was actually intercepted and applied?

Check customer target adapter/PEP status, exact allow/deny/hold, decision binding, no-bypass, dispatch receipt, readback and recovery authority.

Do not infer: NOT_WIRED is not protected; approval is not a verified target side effect.

Owner view: Enforcement and Governance

GOVERNANCE · REPORTS · VERIFICATION

From qualified findings to accountable decisions

Decisions, human authorization, native enforcement and report verification are separate responsibilities. This is where assurance influences business action without overstating what was tested.

Human decision packet

What to present: the action/consequence and environment, exact source-backed code path, relevant policy and IAM state, DAI unknowns, applicable controls and actual test status.

Who decides: an authorized reviewer may approve, reject, request more evidence, defer or revoke within actual HAIEC/customer workflows. Record reviewer, reason, scope and as-of date.

A policy approval is not a target-side allow/deny witness. Do not use the workbook to submit real approval.

Verification and receipt lineage

Inspect: selected exact Evaluation ID, frozen source snapshot, analyzer execution, claim/evidence links, report scope, package/receipt integrity and public/private/revoked state.

Do not equate: receipt integrity with certification or independent proof of every upstream provider claim. Missing sources and contradictory facts should remain visible.

ASSURANCE CONTINUITY

Seven questions for a defensible governance review

  1. Which business action, system, target and environment were in scope?
  2. Which source-qualified paths were found, and which were candidate-only?
  3. What approved policy governed the consequence, and what grants existed?
  4. Which delegated-choice questions and nine DAI facets remain unresolved?
  5. Which controls and tests actually ran, with what observed outcomes?
  6. What human decision, actual target guard and effect proof exist, if any?
  7. Which report/receipt records preserve the result, and what events trigger reassessment?
