# HAIEC — Positioning, USP, Four-Word Messaging, Search Strategy & Curiosity-Led Story System

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** EDITORIAL PROPOSAL / current source-reconciled guidance. Not a rebrand, production claim, site release or independent comparative performance benchmark.  
**Purpose:** Governing copy and positioning supplement for /platform and /products, reusable for later audited pages. Preserve existing HAIEC company identity, category, tagline and visual investments.

## Executive decision

**Do not compete on feature volume, rule count, “autonomous AI security,” or generic continuous protection.** The field already markets inventory, observability, runtime intervention and policy-driven agent governance. Claiming to be the only company that sees or governs agents would be both vulnerable and hard to prove.

**Compete on the buyer's evidence problem:** When an AI system acts through real privileges, **what can it cause, what was it allowed to decide, what do code and observation prove, and what evidence supports an accountable release/operating decision?**

**Category:** Evidence-Bound Assurance.  
**Four-word product description:** **Deterministic AI Action Assurance** (Deterministic=1 AI=2 Action=3 Assurance=4). This is a category descriptor, **not** an overall tagline or statement of universal enforcement.  
**Primary buyer-facing promise:** **Know what your AI can cause. Decide with evidence.**  
**Brand tagline — unchanged:** **High assurance in every consequence.**  
**Short differentiation line:** **HAIEC turns disconnected evidence about AI actions, authority and consequence paths into bounded, reproducible assurance and accountable decisions.**  
**Opposition frame, four words:** **Evidence Over AI Assumptions** (Evidence=1 Over=2 AI=3 Assumptions=4). Use as an editorial eyebrow or supporting campaign line, not as a claim that all evidence is independently complete.  
**Four-word action prompt, setup stage:** **Check my HAIEC setup** (Check=1 my=2 HAIEC=3 setup=4). This maps to actual \`haiec_preflight\`.  
**Four-word action prompt, qualified source stage:** **Assess my AI agent** (Assess=1 my=2 AI=3 agent=4). Only after credential, tenant/system, scope, verified source and consent requirements; the agent should call preflight and not invent missing sources.  
**Four-word exploration prompt:** **Map my agent's actions** (Map=1 my=2 agent's=3 actions=4). Show **supported/known** paths plus unknowns, never imply all paths were discovered.

## Defensible differentiators — ordered

1. **Consequential action path, not just model output or a tool list.** Reconstruct supported edges from agent/tool/API/handler toward relevant resource/effect scope with exact source lineage. **Boundary:** not full uninstrumented end-to-end causality.
2. **Authority is not the same as access.** Requested, policy authorized, effectively granted, code capable and observed planes are separate. DAI adds **delegation comparison** where supported. **Boundary:** platform cannot manufacture customer policy, IAM effective permission or observed execution.
3. **Deterministic assurance; AI-assisted operation.** Canonical evaluator produces bounded, replayable outputs from the **same qualified evidence and method/version**. Assistant interprets and helps gather missing inputs, never authors the official verdict. **Boundary:** same inputs claim requires frozen basis, no arbitrary probabilistic certainty.
4. **Proof boundary is a first-class output.** Establish / partial / unknown / not assessed plus evidence owner, time/coverage and source constraints. This supports enterprise decision-readiness rather than beautifying weak signal. **Boundary:** not a broad guarantee that everything outside evaluated evidence is safe.
5. **Evidence to human decision to verification, with scoped operating extensions.** Reuse native report, approval and receipt owners, distinguish a decision receipt from installed target enforcement and provider-side effect proof. **Boundary:** optional runtime/target-native mediation requires installed customer controls and independent verification.
6. **Agent-native, no duplicate assurance engine.** MCP/REST/IDE can run the same canonical services and guide next action from preflight. **Boundary:** user-authenticated credential, applicable sources, permissions and consents remain needed; the agent's own assertions do not become HAIEC evidence.

**Competitive positioning, non-superlative:** Compared with governance platforms' policy/registry emphasis, tracing platforms' events/evaluations and runtime agents' intervention emphasis, HAIEC should *emphasize* implementation-grounded consequence/authority/evidence reconciliation and bounded decision artifacts. **DO NOT say “only,” “more comprehensive than X,” “superior detection,” “stronger protection,” “number one,” or “first” absent a dated, reproducible side-by-side benchmark.** These markets overlap. “Superior” should be *demonstrated* with a better customer proof experience, not asserted.

## Veritasium-inspired explanatory system (original HAIEC execution, NOT an imitation)

Veritasium's high-level educational device is to put an intuitive assumption under pressure, present a concrete counterexample, then resolve it with a grounded model. HAIEC should use that **conceptual teaching mechanic**, not copy someone's script, graphics, graphics style or presentation identity.

**Audience-belief:** “If the agent passed a security scan and has permission to use a tool, we can approve it.”  
**Question:** “What if every check you ran was correct — and the approval was still wrong?”  
**Counterexample:** Fictional customer-support AI has a valid API credential and a code path to process refunds. Its request is to correct a small billing error. The tool permits editing recipient/amount; no qualified evidence establishes the human delegated authority to choose either. “Authenticated” and “code-capable” can be true while “delegation proven” remains UNKNOWN.  
**Reveal:** Different systems answer different questions, not competing true/false responses. Source shows a possible operation, IAM a possible grant, policy a condition, runtime an observed call; **none alone proves delegated authority or confirmed target effect**.  
**Mechanism:** Show a native-readable “decision anatomy”: **Request** / **Policy** / **Grant** / **Code** / **Observation**, with a separate **Delegation** comparison and a **Consequence** node. It is an analytical comparison, **not a causal five-stage pipeline**.  
**Resolution:** HAIEC evaluates only supported, qualified facts, surfaces the missing delegated approval/observation as its bounded outcome and shows what a human or connected source could supply.  
**Payoff:** The decision becomes more defensible because teams know **what is established, what is not, and where the proof stops**.  
**Action:** Inspect an illustrative sample → check your own setup via MCP → scope an Enterprise POC for consequential approval.

**Never use a fictional successful “block” or “refund prevented” animation unless it's prominently labeled conceptual.** A real runtime effect is distinct evidence. Use one synthetic example only across Platform and Products so visitors build a durable mental model.

### 90-second animated editorial outline
| Time | Visual motion | Message/voiceover theme | Honest evidence lock |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10s | “PASS” badges for one scanner and one credential slide in; last question remains incomplete | “Everything we checked passed. Is that enough to approve an agent?” | badges are *illustrative independent facts* |
| 10–23s | Support agent points to refund API; amount/recipient glow, not the whole page | “The API can be valid even if the business choice was never delegated.” | no real transaction |
| 23–43s | Five plane lenses appear **side by side**, not left-to-right waterfall | “They answer different questions.” | each claim independent |
| 43–57s | Delegation lens reveals UNKNOWN; consequence path remains potential, not executed | “That missing link should remain visible.” | UNKNOWN not FAIL/PASS |
| 57–75s | Bounded evaluation card with evidence refs and “REVIEW” for this **fictional** scoped example | “Deterministic evaluation preserves the boundary.” | do not generalize synthetic result |
| 75–90s | Human decision and report/receipt conceptual handoff; real CTAs revealed | “Know what your AI can cause. Decide with evidence.” | approval record ≠ target-native enforcement |

For page usage use a **12–18 second condensed user-directed scene** with replay / pause and accessible semantic companion text. A full 90-second story belongs on a deeper explainer/video route or expanded dialog, not autoplay hero. Reduced-motion users get a still figure and the same copy. Do **not** synthesize live telemetry.

## Copy QA: block these before publication

- “Our model checks your model” (incorrect; deterministic evaluators and evidence)
- “Verify every AI action” (overclaims observation/effect)
- “Your AI is safe/approved/secure if ALLOW” (outcome limited to evaluated scope)
- “All agents/tools/permissions discovered” (requires source coverage and denominator)
- “Approval automatically enforces policy” (requires installed target PEP)
- “Always-on monitoring with no setup” (requires real source and qualified telemetry)
- “No signup or credentials needed to connect HAIEC MCP” (key/auth applies)
- “All standalone tools automatically write dashboard Assurance Evaluations” (held/nonpipeline)
- “Only HAIEC offers runtime protection, governance or action mapping” (competitors do)
- “Guaranteed compliance/certification” (framework mapping and research are distinct)
- “Agent just asks HAIEC to install itself and scan every repo” (unsupported without compatible MCP client, user key, verified source and scopes)
