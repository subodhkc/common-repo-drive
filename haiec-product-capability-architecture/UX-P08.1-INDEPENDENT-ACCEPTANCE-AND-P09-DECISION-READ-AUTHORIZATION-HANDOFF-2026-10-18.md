## 3. NEW P09-G01 — P1 full decision packet disclosure via existing list and detail GETs

**Source proof at HAIEC Main `5cd6875...`:**
- `lib/ai-inventory/my-work.ts::assembleMyWorkInbox` **correctly** defines the full-packet entitlement policy: `viewerRole in ['admin','owner'] || requestedBy===viewerUserId || assignedReviewerId===viewerUserId`. Non-entitled org members get `detailRestricted:true`, null request CAS digest, empty `allowedOperations/prohibitedOperations/requiredConditions`, null rationale. This is the explicit E6.2 least-privilege product contract.
- **Detail GET** `app/api/inventory/systems/[id]/deployment-decisions/[decisionId]/route.ts`: validates logged-in org and exact `systemId`+decision org, but returns `{...withEff, history}` with all record fields. It **does not** inspect viewer role, `requestedBy`, or `assignedReviewerId` and returns full scope/history to *any member of the organization who knows the decision ID*.
- **List GET** `app/api/inventory/systems/[id]/deployment-decisions/route.ts`: validates logged-in org and `systemId`, loads **all versions** of decision records, and returns `...withEffectiveState(r,ctx)` for every record **without** redaction by requester/reviewer/admin entitlement. It may reveal other members' allowed/prohibited operations, review rationale/digests/conditions and metadata. Neither route crosses the org boundary according to current code, and mutating `/decide` has separate reviewer/CAS/SoD checks.

**Risk:** the My Work UI's conscientious `detailRestricted` presentation does not provide actual confidentiality when other first-party GET routes expose full packets. No live exploitation tested; this is a code-confirmed missing authorization/redaction policy rather than a claimed incident.

**P09 FIRST TASK (before rich master-detail UI):**
1. Make one **server-side canonical decision-read entitlement projection/helper** shared by My Work and both GET routes (or reuse/encapsulate exactly the E6.2 predicate), with tested active role membership and exact org/system/decision binding; avoid storing rights in JS frontend or trusting caller-supplied `role/requester`.
2. **List GET:** preserve legitimate org-wide summary visibility and existing UX/portfolio consumers, but return **summary-only** for non-entitled users; withhold exact permitted/prohibited operations, conditions, rationale, CAS digests and sensitive history; include an explicit `detailRestricted` status. Consider field-allowlist instead of spreading entire record to prevent newly added fields leaking by default. Preserve all-version history *only within authorized detail* and version semantics; do not silently collapse latest `requestKey` contract where screens rely on it.
3. **Detail GET:** full scope + events only if viewer is owner/admin, requester or assigned reviewer under existing E6.2 rules; else bounded 403/404 or summary-only (choose one canonical product contract), including non-disclosing unauthorized/no-record behavior where appropriate. Maintain authorized reviewer's **fresh** full decision fetch before presenting a packet.
4. Keep `POST`, assignment, `/decide`, revocation and server CAS/SoD authorization semantics unchanged. Do not break correct existing authorized review/gov pages. If another route shares full decision records, include a minimal read-surface inventory, not a new authorization platform.
5. Tests: non-entitled org member/list/detail GET cannot see raw scopes/rationale/digests/events; requester, assigned reviewer, admin/owner see exact intended full scope; other org/foreign system not enumerated; changing assignment removes former reviewer rights; no stale member role grants; existing legitimate governance screen remains functional.
