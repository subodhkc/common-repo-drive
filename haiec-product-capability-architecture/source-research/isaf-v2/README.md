**Research and product truth are different.** The source paper defines the ISAF v2 research framework. HAIEC's current product source establishes actual shipped functionality and integration availability. Do not rewrite the research to match convenience in the product, and do not claim completed production support for every research construct.

Key research distinctions to preserve:

- Historical nine instruction-stack abstraction layers 0 through 8 are not interchangeable with modern v2 prompt/context influence sources or the Logger's own implementation-specific layer numbers.
- v2 uses seven related research constructs: Instruction Stack, Consequence Path, five independent Evidence Planes, Delegation Relation, Evidence Spine, Evaluation Epoch and Proof Frontier.
- Delegation is orthogonal to the five evidence planes, not another evidence plane. Technical access does not itself grant business decision authority.
- The 78-checkpoint research catalog is separate from HAIEC's system-specific control catalog.
- Source capability, an evaluated control, observed runtime operation and verified business effect are distinct claims. Useful first-pass findings should not be suppressed simply because additional evidence is unavailable.
- Reference-suite qualification, hard-negative testing, applied exercise evidence and independent held-out validation have different meanings.

**Website presentation:** Light theme by default, customer-focused copy, no internal or fictional-status banners, and no em dashes. Link the v2 research framework under Resources > Learn. Preserve the existing `/isaf` Logger page and link the two pages appropriately.
