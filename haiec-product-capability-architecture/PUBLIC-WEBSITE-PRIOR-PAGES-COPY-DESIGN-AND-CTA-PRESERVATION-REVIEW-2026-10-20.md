## Non-removable CTA priorities

**Enterprise** leads to a real POC qualification form, not a misleading $999 checkout; **self-service** leads to a verified signup/checkout trial; **firm** routes to custom professional scoping; **technical buyer** can inspect sample evidence and connect authenticated MCP. Never delete valid paths just because a new hero gives one of them lower visual priority.

- Preserve Analytics conversion and checkout events and plan/billing/trial query parameters, real form validation and CAPTCHA, authorization qualifiers, support/cancel conditions, source docs and sample artifact links.
- Preserve existing Homepage animations/terminals and carefully scoped replacement of hero only when separately approved; public theme provider shared, not per page.
- Retain actual product detail in accessible HTML/accordions rather than reducing every page to 3 cards from a prototype. Ensure essential SEO copy SSR, important deep links and FAQ visible without simulation.
- Keep three pillars and six independently selectable capabilities (Discover, Evaluate, Authorize, Monitor, Protect, Verify); five evidence planes are peers, DAI delegated authority orthogonal.
- All sample scenarios conspicuously synthetic. A source-based possible action is not observed execution; canonical disposition is not proven target-side blocking.
